<b>Abstract</b><br>
We present high angular resolution (0.19 arcsec or ≈24 pc) Atacama Large Millimeter/submillimeter Array observations of the <SUP>12</SUP>CO(3-2) line emission of the galaxy NGC 4751. The data provide evidence for the presence of a central supermassive black hole (SMBH). Assuming a constant mass-to-light ratio (M/L), we infer a SMBH mass M<SUB>BH</SUB> = 3.43<SUP>+0.45</SUP><SUB>−0.44</SUB> [stat, 3σ] <SUP>+0.22</SUP><SUB>−0.64</SUB> [sys] × 10<SUP>9</SUP> M<SUB>⊙</SUB> and a F160W filter stellar M/L = 2.68 ± 0.11 [stat, 3σ] <SUP>+0.10</SUP><SUB>−0.80</SUB> [sys] M<SUB>⊙</SUB>/L<SUB>⊙,F160W</SUB>, where the first uncertainties are statistical and the second systematic. Assuming a linearly spatially varying M/L, we infer M<SUB>BH</SUB> = 2.79<SUP>+0.75</SUP><SUB>−0.57</SUB> [stat, 3σ] <SUP>+0.75</SUP><SUB>−0.45</SUB> [sys] × 10<SUP>9</SUP> M<SUB>⊙</SUB>. We also present SMBH mass estimates using the Jeans Anisotropic Modelling (JAM) method and Very Large Telescope SINFONI stellar kinematics. Assuming a cylindrically aligned velocity ellipsoid, we infer M<SUB>BH</SUB> = (2.52 ± 0.36) × 10<SUP>9</SUP> M<SUB>⊙</SUB>, while assuming a spherically aligned velocity ellipsoid we infer M<SUB>BH</SUB> = (3.24 ± 0.87) × 10<SUP>9</SUP> M<SUB>⊙</SUB>. The SMBH mass assuming a constant M/L is statistically consistent with that of the spherically aligned JAM model, whereas the mass assuming a linearly varying M/L is consistent with both JAM models within the uncertainties. Our derived masses are larger than, and inconsistent with, one previous stellar dynamical measurement using the Schwarzschild orbit-superposition method and the same SINFONI kinematics.
